About this prototype

Bay: 24 mm · Frame roof: 16.6 mm · Themed relief: 17.2 mm · Plate: 7.6 mm. Heights above the base, without feet.

36 Piantor positions. Original KLP Lamé meshes. Units: mm. The directory includes all 12 component groups. A fine line connects the active visible part to its entry.

Nominal CAD study. Zero geometric DRC violations; 104 unconnected items per PCB. Both battery profiles fit the modeled aperture. Magnetic frames, printed threads, actual cables/contacts and hardware operation require physical tests. Commercial components use approximate envelopes.
