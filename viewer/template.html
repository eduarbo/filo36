About this prototype

Bay: 24 mm · Frame roof: 16.6 mm · Themed relief: 17.2 mm · Plate: 7.6 mm. Heights above the base, without feet.

36 Piantor positions. Original KLP Lamé meshes. Units: mm. Labels group repeated parts and follow visible surfaces; the Parts list includes all 12 layers.

Nominal CAD study. Unrouted PCB. Battery opening: 0.15 mm per cradle side and 0.22 mm to pads; DRC unresolved. Cables, contacts, retention and physical fit untested. Electronics use approximate envelopes; switch bodies, keycap seating, screws and feet are illustrative.
