# Explore the assembly

**[Open the 3D viewer →](https://eduarbo.github.io/filo36/)**

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use two fingers to pan. Pick a half or a standard view; hide layers to inspect the inside. **Explode parts** separates layers visually without changing the saved assembly.

**Customize** selects KLP variants by key, row or thumb cluster and frame styles/colors per half. **Save configuration** and **Load JSON** transfer choices to another browser or [FreeCAD](customize.md#save-a-configuration-for-freecad). **Reset view** preserves your choices; **Default configuration** restores the original parts.

**Download offline HTML** saves the entire viewer, including geometry, code and licenses. No network requests are needed to run that file. It is larger than a static picture because it contains the supported keycap meshes. First load may take longer on a phone.

**Download assembled GLB** exports all installed parts in their assembled positions, including the selected keycaps and frames. Hidden layers and exploded offsets are viewing aids and do not remove or displace parts in the exported assembly. GLB uses meters and retains source/license metadata; FreeCAD/STEP is the route for solid editing.

The viewer is a nominal CAD study. See [dimensions and limitations](cad.md), [customization rules](customize.md) and [parts](parts.md). Browser checks include desktop and a narrow touch viewport; physical phone and hardware acceptance remain separate.
